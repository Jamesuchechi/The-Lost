# The Lost: Ideas Backlog

This file tracks design ideas, sensory enhancements, and potential future features without changing the core active phase scope.

## Sensory & Environmental Immersion
- **Dynamic 2D Line-of-Sight Shadows:** Raycasted dynamic 2D shadows behind trees, boulders, and walls creating blind spots behind cover.
- **Wind Direction & Scent Dynamics:** Dynamic ambient wind carrying scent cones downwind and masking player approach upwind.
- **Dynamic Foliage & Terrain Reaction:** Grass and bushes parting/swaying when entities pass through; visual disturbance in fog before enemies emerge.
- **Off-Screen Acoustic Cues:** Directional audio ripples on HUD edges when snaps, steps, or growls occur just outside vision range.
- **Panic & Heartbeat Feedback:** Audio low-pass muffling, ragged breathing, and subtle vignette pulsing during low stamina exhaustion or active hunts.

## Physical & Tactical Mechanics
- **Dynamic Surface Effects:** Mud/water reducing speed and increasing noise; tall grass boosting concealment; dry leaves crunching loudly.
- **Droppable & Throwable Torches:** Throwing or dropping torches onto brush to create temporary walls of flame and ward off predators.
- **Blood & Scent Trails:** Bleeding from injury leaves a scent trail wolves can track with heightened speed.

## Visual Fidelity & Realism
- **Realistic Top-Down Visuals:** High-fidelity realistic top-down textures (detailed mossy ground, realistic tree canopies, photorealistic bark, dynamic shadows, detailed animal anatomies and hunter gear) replacing flat minimalist shapes.
- **Dynamic Environmental Particles:** Drifting volumetric fog patches, swirling leaves, rain ripples, and torch ember particle systems.
- **Diegetic World Artifacts:** Discarded board game relics (cracked tokens, bloodstained dice, carved markers) embedded in the forest floor.
